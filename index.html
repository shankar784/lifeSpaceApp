What's inside

Six things that usually live in six different apps.

Journal

Write entries with photos, audio and tags. Keep separate spaces for different parts of your life.

Document scanner

Scan notes, receipts and paperwork with your camera. Multi-page scans are saved straight into your space.

Gallery

Images, videos, audio and files stay grouped with the entry they belong to, so nothing gets orphaned.

Calendar

Every entry laid out by date, so you can go back to any day and pick up the thread.

Tasks

Track what needs doing next to the notes it relates to, instead of in a separate to-do app.

PIN & biometric lock

The app opens with a PIN or your fingerprint, so a borrowed phone doesn't mean a borrowed diary.

Private is the default, not a setting.

LifeSpace is built around the assumption that what you write down is nobody else's business.

  • Locked by default. PIN and biometric unlock are part of the app, not an upsell.
  • No account required to write. Open the app and start — no sign-up wall.
  • Your entries, your export. Anything you put in, you can take back out.
