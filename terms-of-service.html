5 Encryption and data loss

LifeSpace encrypts your diary database and your media files on your device. The key that unlocks them is generated on your device and held in your device's secure storage. We never hold that key and it is never sent to our servers.

Your key travels with your backup

If you turn on Google Drive backup, a copy of that key is stored in your own Google Drive, in a private folder that only LifeSpace can see. This is what makes a backup usable: without the key, an encrypted backup could never be opened again, not even by you.

So the key goes to your Google account — not to us. You do not need to write it down or keep it anywhere yourself.

What this means for recovery

With a Drive backup: you can restore your diary on a new device by signing in with the same Google account. Losing your phone does not mean losing your entries.

Without a backup: your entries exist only on that one device. If you lose it, uninstall the app, clear its data or reset the device, they are gone permanently. We hold no copy and there is no support override that can bring them back.

Please turn on backup before you rely on LifeSpace for anything you would be upset to lose.

Keep your Google account secure

Because your key is kept in your Google Drive, the safety of your backup is the safety of that Google account. Anyone who gains access to it may be able to restore your diary. We strongly recommend turning on two-step verification for the account you back up to.

Your PIN is separate

The PIN and biometric lock protect the app on your device. Your PIN is stored on that device only, as a one-way hash — it is never uploaded, we never see it, and we cannot reset or recover it.

The PIN is not what encrypts your backup, so forgetting it does not destroy your entries if you have a backup: reinstalling and restoring lets you set a new one.

Please verify that your backups exist and check them from time to time.
